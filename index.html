About simulation behavior

“Starting population” is initialization data and Reset restores it. Calibration uses Auto-balance as a seed, then pre-simulates the non-invasive ecosystem to estimate its healthy long-term equilibrium. Ecology now resolves monthly while authoring values stay annual. Producers regenerate edible biomass from habitat capacity rather than surviving producer count. Juveniles are intentionally not modeled yet. Consumer births are distributed across monthly ticks and enter the active population immediately after their birth month, so they can participate in feeding and predation on the following month. A yearly breeding-pool cap keeps those newborns from increasing reproductive eligibility until the following simulated year. Predation resolves before surviving prey feed. Producers use only starting population, carrying capacity, annual regrowth, edible biomass, and predation rating in the editor. Monthly diagnostics expose the math step by step. Food allocation now prioritizes survival before reproduction: basic survival rations are funded first, and only remaining surplus can make animals reproduction-eligible. Reproduction Surplus sets both the extra food cost and the maximum reproductive tendency. Species marked invasive are excluded from calibration. The run-controls checkbox can include or exclude all invasive species from simulation; turning it on explicitly introduces them at their authored Starting Population at the current year, while turning it off removes them from active simulation. Native Expected Population is estimated from the mature pre-invasion ecosystem. After calibration, Year 0 continues from the exact final state of the mature native burn-in rather than reconstructing a fresh ecosystem from averaged populations; the entire native state is rescaled proportionally so the anchor Expected Population matches the authored anchor. Invasive Starting Population remains its authored introduction size. Expected Population is not a cap: populations may overshoot it freely. For prey below its frozen Auto-Balance Encounter Baseline, encounterability declines in proportion to current/baseline abundance, making scarce prey harder for predators to locate before they are eaten to extinction. Consumer populations below one individual are now treated as extinct and set to zero at the end of the monthly tick.

About population history

Each species gets a categorical population timeline relative to its population-health reference. You can branch from any recorded year: the simulator restores that year’s state, discards the later timeline/diagnostics, and continues forward from there. Native species use calibrated Expected Population. Invasive species use Expected Population Override when supplied; otherwise they use their authored Reference Species. Extinct = 0; Critical = above 0 but below 10%; Scarce = 10–50%; Stable = 50–150%; Abundant = 150–250%; Overpopulated = 250% or more. Labels below each bar mark every year in which the rating changes.

Visualization output

Turns simulation state into an illustration brief. Population uses Expected Population. Body condition uses food sufficiency. Babies, carcasses (natural + starvation deaths), and remains (predation deaths) are compared as per-capita annual rates against the native healthy calibration distribution. “Normal” is the middle 80% of healthy calibration years (10th–90th percentile), so the ecosystem defines its own normal variation. Invasive species have no pre-invasion healthy baseline, so they use pooled native consumer per-capita event rates as a fallback.

Analysis range

Analysis Range controls only Yearly and Monthly Diagnostics. It does not change Population History, Current State, or Visualization Output. Set both From and To years (or use Latest 25 / View year) to populate the diagnostic tables.

Yearly diagnostics

Species to display

One row per species per year. Set an explicit Analysis Range before this table loads. Flows such as births and deaths are annual totals; survival-only and reproduction-eligible are monthly averages; population is the end-of-year active population.
